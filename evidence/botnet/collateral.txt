Headline collateral:
  CSMS forced session-stops during the window: 106 - 85 = 21
  Porto charging power driven to 0 W (its live sessions terminated on quarantine).

Measurement caveat (a finding, not a defect of the attack):
  The sim's evcharge_sim_sessions_total{result="interrupted"} counter stayed 0 because a
  session cut by a CSMS-side change-availability/disconnect is recorded by the simulator as
  a normal stop, not an "interrupted" one. The honest collateral signal is therefore the
  central-side CSMS stopped-session delta (21) and the per-city charging-power collapse,
  not the sim interrupted counter. The instrument should also count CSMS-forced stops.
